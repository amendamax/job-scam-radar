10 Red Flags You're Talking to a Job Scammer

The fake recruiter will bombard you with amazing salary promises for very little work (like watching YouTube videos). The goal is to make you greedy before asking you to deposit crypto to 'unlock' tasks.

They message you out of the blue from an unknown number on WhatsApp or Telegram, claiming to be from a major recruitment agency, but their phone number is from a completely different country.

Their jobs justify why they cannot meet you. Typical roles include engineers on remote offshore rigs, military personnel deployed abroad, or doctors on foreign missions.

Suddenly, an emergency arises: they need urgent medical funds, their bank accounts are frozen, or they need cash for a plane ticket to see you. This is when the scam is triggered.
